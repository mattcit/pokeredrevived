GAMEPLAY

Common Gen 1 Bugfixes

Improved Gym Leader, Elite Four and Giovanni/Rival teams
	Added some counter-type Pokemon to each team to make beating them with a single type harder

Improved EXP ALL to give more EXP and made it available earlier (swapped places with ITEMFINDER)

Added more trainers to Viridian Forest to make early game grinding less annoying
	Also added new trainers to Route 4 (between Mt Moon and Cerulean City) to help with the level gap before Misty
		NOTE: Made a slight map change to Route 4 so that after entering Cerulean you can go back to fight the trainers on Route 4, but you still can't go back into Mt Moon


POKEMON

All 151 Pokemon are available without trading (all trade evolutions are now level up evolutions)

All wild Pokemon encounters have been adjusted to include more variety

*All Pokemon learnsets have been updated/rebalanced


MOVES

Added moves from later gens to fill out and balance out each Type
	Example: Added Mud Slap and Mud Shot because there were no lower level Ground type moves

Rebalanced HM moves so that they all are valuable in battle
	Example: Flash now lowers accuracy and causes confusion, Cut is Grass type and can flinch etc

HM moves can be forgotten

Karate Chop is now a Fighting type move
	Removed High Crit chance to balance it better as a low level Fighting type move

Low Kick has a lower Flinch chance
	To balance it better as a low level Fighting type move

Razor Wind and Sky Attack are now High Crit chance moves (like in later gens)

Increased Glare accuracy to 90 (like in later gens)
	Ekans/Arbok deserve a more useful signature move

Dizzy Punch now has Confusion side effect (like in later gens)

Rock Throw accuracy increased to 90 (like in later gens)
